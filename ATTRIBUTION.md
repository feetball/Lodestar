# Attribution

Lodestar stands on open astronomical data. This file credits every third-party data source that
ships inside the app and states the license each one is used under.

## Catalog data

The star, deep-sky, constellation and Milky Way catalogs live in
`LodestarKit/Sources/LodestarKit/Resources/` and are generated by `Tools/build_catalogs.py` from
upstream files that are pinned to the exact commits and sha256 digests recorded in
`Tools/sources.lock.json`.

### HYG Database — `stars.json`

- **Title:** HYG Database, version 4.1 (Hipparcos, Yale, Gliese)
- **Author:** astronexus, maintainer of the HYG Database
- **URL:** <https://www.astronexus.com/hyg> · repository
  <https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database> (an archive; development continues at
  <https://codeberg.org/astronexus/hyg>)
- **Used:** `hyg/CURRENT/hygdata_v41.csv`
- **Commit:** `c7f7f883fe678cc7680169a50ccd7dcc49b060ce`
- **License:** CC BY-SA 4.0, as stated in the repository's `LICENSE` and `hyg/README.md`
- **Compiled by upstream from:** the Hipparcos catalogue (ESA), the Yale Bright Star Catalog
  (5th ed., Hoffleit and Warren) and the Gliese–Jahreiss Catalogue of Nearby Stars; proper names
  follow the IAU Working Group on Star Names and the IAU NameExoWorlds campaigns.
- **Changes made for Lodestar:** converted from CSV to compact JSON; kept every star brighter than
  magnitude 6.5 plus every named star, dropped the Sun and unnamed entries that carry neither a
  Hipparcos nor a Harvard Revised number; right ascension converted from hours to degrees;
  Bayer designations converted to Greek letters with superscripts; the right ascension of Hipparcos
  stars corrected for the proper-motion term missing in HYG's epoch propagation (up to 18
  arcseconds for the nearest, fastest stars); fields renamed and rounded; stars sorted by
  magnitude.

- **Title:** OpenNGC, a license-friendly NGC/IC objects database
- **Author:** Mattia Verga (copyright 2023) and contributors Patrick Chevalley, Christian Dersch,
  Jean-Sebastien Dieu and Sebastian Godelet
- **URL:** <https://github.com/mattiaverga/OpenNGC>
- **Used:** `database_files/NGC.csv` and `database_files/addendum.csv`, release `v20260501`
- **Commit:** `36cb178a0f69dba8bfc03a99c10512831edf1c6b`
- **License:** CC BY-SA 4.0, as stated in the repository's `README.md`, `.reuse/dep5` and
  `LICENSES/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt`
- **Changes made for Lodestar:** converted from CSV to compact JSON; selected all 110 Messier
  objects, every object with a common name, every catalogue object with V ≤ 10.0 (or B ≤ 10.5 when

  were added (see "Names added by Lodestar"), and the name "Flame Nebula" was removed from IC 434,

### Names added by Lodestar

`Tools/build_catalogs.py` adds a small table of widely used common names that OpenNGC lacks (for
example "Heart Nebula" for IC 1805, "Pinwheel Galaxy" for M101, and the acronyms "LMC" and "SMC").
These are plain facts about how objects are commonly called and carry no third-party rights.

## Models and algorithms compiled into the code

These are published scientific models and numeric tables implemented in `LodestarKit`; no data
files are shipped for them.

### World Magnetic Model 2025 — `Geomagnetism/WMM2025Coefficients.swift`

- **Title:** The US/UK World Magnetic Model for 2025–2030 (WMM2025)
- **Authors:** NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI) and the British
  Geological Survey (BGS)
- **URL:** <https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/world-magnetic-model> · coefficients
  <https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/2024-12/WMM2025COF.zip> (sha256
  `2e76569370d081f2cd7919490218bd094ca9afde347b198eff5621e0af460d03`), report
  <https://doi.org/10.25923/prbc-s316>
- **License:** a work of the US Government, in the public domain in the United States; credited
  here as NOAA requests.
- **Use:** magnetic declination (to turn magnetic headings into true headings when the phone's
  true-north reference is unavailable) and expected field strength (to detect magnetic
  interference). Valid 2025.0–2030.0; replace the coefficients with WMM2030 when it is released
  (`Tools/generate_wmm_coefficients.py`).

### Solar-system theories — `Ephemeris/`

- **VSOP87B** planetary theory, P. Bretagnon & G. Francou, *Astronomy & Astrophysics* 202, 309
  (1988), files from IMCCE (`ftp.imcce.fr/pub/ephem/planets/vsop87/`, also CDS VizieR VI/81),
  truncated to 1,002 terms (`Tools/ephemeris/gen_planetary_theory.py`).
- **Lunar theory:** ELP-2000/82 as truncated by Jean Meeus, *Astronomical Algorithms*, 2nd ed.
  (Willmann-Bell, 1998), chapter 47, with coefficients cross-checked against the MIT-licensed
  implementations `soniakeys/meeus` and `commenthol/astronomia`.
- **Nutation, precession, sidereal time, aberration, refraction:** IAU 1980 nutation (Meeus
  Table 22.A), IAU 1976 precession (Lieske et al. 1977), IAU 1982 sidereal time, Sæmundsson
  and Bennett refraction formulas, all as given by Meeus.
- **Planetary magnitudes:** A. Mallama & J. L. Hilton, "Computing apparent planetary magnitudes for
  The Astronomical Almanac", *Astronomy and Computing* 25, 10 (2018), arXiv:1808.01973.

## Reproducing and updating

```
python3 -I Tools/build_catalogs.py --out LodestarKit/Sources/LodestarKit/Resources --cache <dir>
```

downloads the pinned files, verifies their digests and rewrites the four data files. When
`--refresh-lock` moves a source to a newer commit, update the commit hashes in this file; the
script warns when they no longer match, and also warns when an upstream license or readme file
changed so the terms above can be re-checked.
